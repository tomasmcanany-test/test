In brief Rice et al. show that novel unicellular projections found in the postgonal sheath of Drosophila eugracilis express the key trichome transcription factor shavenbaby and 14 genes of the known larval trichome genetic network. Expression of shavenbaby in the projection-lacking species Drosophila melanogaster is sufﬁcient to induce this novel trait.
