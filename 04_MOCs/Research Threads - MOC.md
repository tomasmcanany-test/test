# Research Threads — MOC

> **Synthesis map of the main research threads in the Drosophila Terminalia vault.**
> Every source paper (19) and literature review (19) has been read and grouped below

> (wikilinked to the source notes in `01_Sources`), its driving question, and the
> deeper reviews in `08_Reviews` that unpack the figures.

## How the threads fit together

All four threads are faces of one core question: **how do the rapidly evolving,
sexually dimorphic terminalia of *Drosophila* arise developmentally, and how do they
evolve at the level of gene regulation?** A conserved developmental toolkit — the
**genital imaginal disc**, patterned by **Hox genes (Abd-B)** and the sex-determination
output **Doublesex (dsx)** — is repeatedly redeployed to build species-specific and
sex-specific structures. The threads differ in emphasis:

## Thread 1 — Morphological novelty by gene-regulatory-network (GRN) co-option

**Driving question.** How do genuinely *new* structures originate? Answer: largely by
**co-opting pre-existing gene regulatory networks**, at the level of enhancers and
transcription-factor binding sites, rather than inventing new genes.

**Primary papers**

- [[Co-option of an Ancestral Hox-Regulated Network Underlies a Recently Evolved Morphological Novelty]] (Glassford et al., 2015) — the **cornerstone**: the male **posterior lobe** evolved by re-deploying the Hox-regulated network of the larval posterior spiracle (10 shared genes incl. 7 TFs; 7 shared enhancers).
- [[A Notch signal required for a morphological novelty in Drosophila has antecedent functions in genital disc eversion]] (Shodja et al., 2025) — the posterior-lobe Notch signaling center **emerged from a pre-existing role in genital-disc eversion** (via the apical ECM); divergence maps to discrete *Delta* enhancers.
- [[Co-option of the trichome-forming network initiated the evolution of a morphological novelty in Drosophila eugracilis]] (Rice et al., 2024) — a dense male genital structure evolved by **co-opting the conserved *svb* trichome network**.
- [[Resolving between novelty and homology in the rapidly evolving phallus of Drosophila]] (Rice et al., 2023) — developmental analysis distinguishes **homologues from independent novelties**; the same GRN can be redeployed to different primordia (convergent/parallel traits).

## Thread 2 — Sex determination and the *doublesex* control of sexual dimorphism

**Driving question.** How does the conserved sex-determination switch **Doublesex (dsx)**,
working with Hox positional information (Scr/Abd-B), impose male vs. female identity on a
shared primordium to produce dimorphic structures?

**Primary papers**

- [[Genetic control and evolution of sexually dimorphic characters in Drosophila]] (Kopp et al., 2000) — foundational: *bric à brac (bab)* as determinant of dimorphic abdominal pigmentation; **cis-regulatory evolution** at *bab*; dsx + Hox integration.
- [[The regulation and evolution of a genetic switch controlling sexually dimorphic traits in Drosophila]] (Williams et al., 2008) — the landmark **Abd-B + DSX genetic switch** at a *bab* enhancer, with a distributed, redundant enhancer architecture.
- [[Evolution of sex-specific traits through changes in HOX-dependent doublesex expression]] (Tanaka et al., 2011) — sex comb built by a **dsx–Scr positive-feedback module**; origin/diversification via gain of a new *dsx* domain.
- [[Evolving doublesex expression correlates with the origin and diversification of male sexual ornaments in the Drosophila immigrans species group]] (Rice et al., 2018) — evolutionary **decoupling of dsx from Scr** correlates with ornament divergence/loss.
- [[Dmrt genes in the development and evolution of sexual dimorphism]] (Kopp, 2012) and [[Evolution of sexual development and sexual dimorphism in insects]] (Hopkins & Kopp, 2021) — reviews framing dsx as a **conserved Dmrt switch with labile upstream inputs**.
- [[Distinct developmental mechanisms underlie the evolutionary diversification in Drosophila: sex combs]] (Tanaka et al., 2009) — convergent sex combs can arise by **distinct developmental mechanisms** (bristle-row rotation via cell rearrangement).

## Thread 3 — Evo-devo of genitalia: rapid divergence, sexual selection & speciation

**Driving question.** Why do genitalia and male sexual characters **evolve so fast and
diverge so dramatically** among closely related species, and what forces (sexual selection,
reproductive isolation) drive it?

**Primary papers**

- [[Evolution of male sexual characters in the Oriental Drosophila melanogaster species group]] (Kopp & True, 2002) — **mosaic, rapid evolution**: different male sexual characters map to the tree with hugely different fit (labile/homoplastic vs. clade-defining).
- [[Co-evolving wing spots and mating displays are genetically separable traits in Drosophila]] (Massey et al., 2020) — a male wing ornament and its courtship display **co-evolve via correlated selection but are genetically separable** (X-linked *optomotor-blind* switch vs. polygenic display).
- [[Evolution of sexual development and sexual dimorphism in insects]] (Hopkins & Kopp, 2021) — general framework for how dimorphic traits evolve **stepwise** (regulatory DNA over coding change).

## Thread 4 — Comparative developmental atlases and standardized nomenclature

**Driving question.** What are the anatomical landmarks, transcription-factor markers, and
developmental time courses needed to *do* comparative, causal evo-devo on the terminalia?

**Primary papers**

- [[A Standardized Nomenclature and Atlas of the Male Terminalia of Drosophila melanogaster]] (Rice et al., 2019) — standardized male names + atlas (community consensus across 15 labs).
- [[A standardized nomenclature and atlas of the female terminalia of Drosophila melanogaster]] (McQueen et al., 2022) — female counterpart with synonyms + 3D models.
- [[An Atlas of Transcription Factors Expressed in Male Pupal Terminalia of Drosophila melanogaster]] (Vincent et al., 2019) — stage-dependent (28 h vs 48 h APF) TF markers; searchable DB.
- [[A developmental atlas of male terminalia across twelve species of Drosophila]] (Urum et al., 2024) — links adult morphology to development and phylogeny across species.
- [[Parallels in the regulatory landscape of dimorphic female and male genital structures in Drosophila melanogaster]] (McQueen et al., 2025) — 29 TFs traced in **both sexes** via HCR; female and male genitalia deploy the same markers in different spatial arrangements (brings female terminalia to parity).
- [[Trichomes on female reproductive tract: rapid diversification and underlying gene regulatory network in Drosophila suzukii and its related species]] (Tanaka et al., 2022) — female reproductive-tract trichome diversification; implicates the *svb* network (female-side co-option).
