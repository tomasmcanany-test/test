## Scope

This knowledge base covers the **development, genetics, and evolution** of the
*Drosophila melanogaster* **terminalia** (external genitalia and analia), with
emphasis on:

- The **genital disc** and **imaginal disc** development
- **Hox genes** and **Abd-B** in posterior patterning
- **Sex determination** and **doublesex** in sexual dimorphism
- **Morphogen signaling** in disc patterning
- **Evolution of genitalia** and **evo-devo**

  divergence?

- What is the relative role of **sexual selection** vs. reproductive isolation
  in genitalia evolution?
- How are conserved morphogen gradients redeployed to generate **morphological
  novelty** in the terminalia?
