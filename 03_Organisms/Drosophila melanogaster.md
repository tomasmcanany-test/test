# Drosophila melanogaster

## Taxonomy

- **Kingdom:** Animalia
- **Phylum:** Arthropoda
- **Class:** Insecta
- **Order:** Diptera
- **Family:** Drosophilidae
- **Genus:** *Drosophila*
- **Species:** *Drosophila melanogaster*

## Key Features

- A small fruit fly, ~2–3 mm, widely used as a **model organism** in genetics,
  development, and evolution.
- Short generation time (~10 days at 25 °C), large numbers of offspring, and
  powerful genetic tools.
- **Sexually dimorphic**, including in the **terminalia/genitalia**.

## Development

- Holometabolous: egg → larva (3 instars) → pupa → adult (**metamorphosis**).
- Adult cuticular structures develop from **imaginal discs**, including the
  **genital disc** that forms the **terminalia**.

## Genetics & Tools

- Four chromosome pairs; compact genome (~180 Mb).
- Extensive genetic toolkit: GAL4/UAS, CRISPR, balancer chromosomes,
  transposon mutagenesis, and a large collection of mutants and transgenic
  lines.
- **Sex determination** via the X:A ratio → **Sxl → tra → dsx** cascade.

## Relevance to Terminalia Research

- The **terminalia** are a classic model for studying **Hox gene** function,
  **sex determination**, and the **evolution of genitalia** (evo-devo).
